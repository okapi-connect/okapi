Privacy Policy

The short version

In short: Okapi keeps everything you save on your own computer. It has no accounts, no servers, no analytics and no ads. We don't collect, sell or share your data.

1. Stored on your device

Your highlights, notes, tags, summaries and settings are stored in your browser's local storage (IndexedDB). We have no server to send them to, and we can't see them.

2. AI runs on your device

Summaries use Chrome's built-in AI (Gemini Nano) and only run when you press Summarize. The text is not stored or sent to any server by Okapi.

3. No accounts, you own your data

No sign-up, no tracking. Export to PDF, Word, text or JSON whenever you like, and delete everything from Settings › Data.
