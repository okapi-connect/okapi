Support Okapi

Okapi is free, and it stays free either way. If it has saved you time, you can leave a tip to help keep it maintained. Tips are optional and never unlock anything.

In India

Pay once, in rupees, by UPI, card or netbanking.

Coming soon

Anywhere in the world

Buy a coffee with a card, Apple Pay or Google Pay. No account needed.

Buy me a coffee

Payments are handled on Razorpay's and Buy Me a Coffee's own pages. Okapi never sees your payment details, and nothing in the extension changes whether you tip or not.

Other ways to help

  • Leave a review on the Chrome Web Store once Okapi is listed.
  • Tell a friend who reads a lot online.
  • Report a bug or suggest a feature on GitHub.
